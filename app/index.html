Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough is enough: there is no difficulty setting, no New Game+, and no mission replay, but free roam carries on after the story. Early estimates put the platinum at around 200 hours, most of it on the 100% checklist. Only one trophy is possibly missable — see the warning below — everything else can be done after the credits.

    ⚠ Possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — in Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed yet whether the trophy needs it, so do not skip it. (GTABase trophy guide.)
